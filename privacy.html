Privacy policy

How We Look After Your Information

We only ask for the details we genuinely need in order to respond to your enquiry. This page explains what we collect, why, and how it is handled.

Last updated: October 2026

1. Who we are

Kamamie Quickfast Homecare provides countrywide home-based care in Kenya. This website is operated by Kamamie Quickfast Homecare. If you have any questions about this policy, you can reach us by calling or messaging +254 711 105620 on WhatsApp.

2. What information we collect

When you submit the enquiry form on this website, we collect only the details needed to respond to you:

  • Your full name
  • A phone number and, optionally, an email address
  • The type of support you are looking for
  • Any short message you choose to include

We do not ask for medical records, diagnoses, or other sensitive personal information through this website, and we ask you not to include them in the message field.

3. How your information is used

We use the information you provide solely to:

  • Respond to your enquiry and discuss your homecare needs
  • Contact you about the support you have asked about
  • Keep a record of enquiries we have received

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information, and we do not use it for unrelated marketing.

4. How enquiry submissions are handled

Enquiries submitted through the form on this website are processed by Formspree, a third-party form-handling service, which delivers the submission to us by email. Formspree processes the data you submit in line with its own privacy terms. We recommend reviewing Formspree's privacy policy for more detail.

When you call or message us directly by phone or WhatsApp, your communication is handled under the terms of those services.

5. How long we keep information

We keep enquiry details for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to you and to maintain a sensible record of our communications. If you would like us to delete your details, please contact us and we will do so where we are able.

6. Your choices

You can contact us at any time to ask what information we hold about you, to correct it, or to ask us to remove it. You are also free to contact us by phone or WhatsApp instead of using the website form.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised “last updated” date.

8. Contact

For any privacy-related questions, please call or message +254 711 105620 on WhatsApp.
