About us

Care That Respects the Person Behind Every Need.

We help people live comfortably and independently at home — with support that is practical, personal and always delivered with respect.

Home is where care works best

Home is familiar. It holds your routines, your things, the people you love. When everyday tasks become harder, being helped at home — rather than somewhere unfamiliar — can make an enormous difference to how a person feels.

That is the thinking behind Kamamie Quickfast Homecare. We provide countrywide home-based care across Kenya, built around the person rather than a schedule. We take time to understand what matters to you, how you like things done, and what independence means for you now.

Our approach is calm and unhurried. We ask before we help, we respect privacy, and we make space for people to keep doing what they still can for themselves. Dignity, we have found, lives in the details.

“Care is not only about what needs doing. It is about how it feels to be helped.”

The Kamamie Quickfast Homecare approach

What guides our work

Four Things We Hold Onto

Simple principles, applied consistently. They shape how we plan support and how we behave in someone's home.

Compassionate Support

Care delivered with warmth, patience and genuine kindness — never rushed.

Personalised Attention

Support shaped around your routines, preferences and the pace that suits you.

Respect and Dignity

Every person treated with courtesy, privacy and respect for their choices.

Dependable Service

Organised, reliable help that you and your family can plan around.

Who we support

Support for Real, Everyday Situations

Older people at home

Company, practical help and reassurance for those who would like to keep living in their own home for as long as possible.

People recovering at home

Gentle, non-medical help with personal care, meals and getting through the day while strength returns.

Families who need a break

Respite support for relatives who are caring for a loved one and need time to rest or work.

Anyone who needs a hand

Practical help with daily tasks and household jobs when life has become a little too much to manage alone.
