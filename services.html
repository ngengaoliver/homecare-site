Our services

The Right Support for Everyday Living

Support that is built around real life at home — practical, personal and delivered with respect. Every arrangement starts with a conversation, so tell us what you need and we will talk through what is possible.

01 — Personal care

Personal Care

Dignified help with the things that matter most. Our carers support washing, dressing, grooming and moving around your home safely — always at your pace, and always with your privacy protected.

  • Help with bathing, dressing and personal grooming
  • Support with continence care and toileting
  • Gentle assistance with moving around the home
Enquire about personal care

02 — Elderly care

Elderly Care & Companionship

Friendly company, conversation and reassurance for older people who would like someone to talk to, share a meal with, or simply have nearby. Loneliness is eased by presence, and presence is something we can offer.

  • Companionship, conversation and shared activities
  • Encouragement with hobbies, reading and gentle walks
  • Reassurance and a friendly, familiar face
Enquire about companionship

03 — Daily activities

Assistance with Daily Activities

A steady hand with the things that fill a day — getting up, moving between rooms, preparing light meals, and getting out for appointments or a little fresh air.

  • Help getting up, dressed and moving about safely
  • Preparing simple, warm meals and drinks
  • Accompanying you to appointments or on short outings
Enquire about daily support

04 — Household support

Household Support

Practical help that keeps a home comfortable. Light cleaning, laundry, tidying and warm meals — the everyday jobs that quietly make a house feel like home again.

  • Light cleaning, dusting and tidying
  • Laundry and changing bed linen
  • Simple meal preparation and kitchen tidy-up
Enquire about household help

05 — Respite care

Respite Care

Thoughtful relief for family members who carry a lot. We step in so unpaid carers can rest, work, attend appointments, or simply take a much-needed break — knowing their loved one is in good hands.

  • Cover for a few hours, a day, or a longer period
  • Support that follows the routine already in place
  • A handover so nothing is missed while you are away
Enquire about respite

06 — Individualised support

Specialised & Individualised Support

Every situation is different, and some need a more considered approach. Tell us what is going on and we will talk it through honestly, arrange what is appropriate, and be clear about what we can and cannot provide.

  • Support planned around a specific situation
  • Availability depends on location and the support required
  • Honest guidance if another service would suit you better
Discuss your needs

A note on what we do

Our focus is day-to-day, non-medical support at home. If you need clinical or nursing care, please tell us — we would rather be honest about what we can and cannot provide than promise something we cannot deliver.

Let’s talk

Not Sure What You Need?

Tell us about your situation and we will help you work out the right level of support. There is no obligation — just an honest conversation.
